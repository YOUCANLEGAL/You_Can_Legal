Slovakia

Slovakia work opportunity — full program information.

Positions & Salary

  • Production Line Worker
  • Packing Operator
  • Age limit: 55
  • Salary: €900–€1,600/month
  • Employer-paid health insurance/social contributions: approximately €380, not deducted from salary

Accommodation, Transport & Tests

  • Dormitory: €80–€150/month deducted
  • Transport depends on factory/place of residence
  • Logic test assembling figures
  • Hand motor-skills test

Documents

  • Preparation: approximately 3–4 weeks
  • Work Permit
  • Invitation Letter
  • Preliminary Employment Contract
  • Accommodation Confirmation
  • Police Clearance Certificate obtained by applicant; recommended about 1.5 months before embassy appointment

Additional Local Costs

  • Super-legalization €30 for listed non-apostille countries
  • Visa fee €90
  • Medical insurance 6 months €150
  • Translations €45–€55
  • At least €1,000 on arrival for registration/police/local translations
  • Flights, visa fee and travel insurance are not covered

Process & Residence

  • Choose package/send passport + CV
  • Video meeting
  • Sign Service Agreement electronically
  • Payment
  • Book official Embassy Appointment
  • Prepare Employment Promise (Prísľub zamestnania)
  • Employer documents sent by DHL
  • Apply at Slovak Embassy; processing follows applicable authority timeline
  • Travel/start Official Employment Contract
  • Apply for Residence Permit (Zamestnanie)
  • Residence permit stated validity: 2 years; plastic card approximately 2–3 weeks

Program Cost & Start

  • €1,800 for work contract + stated 2-year residence permit arrangement
  • Start with clear, high-quality passport scan, all pages in correct order
Important: No sponsorship is provided; the program is paid. Immigration and visa decisions are made by the relevant authorities.
